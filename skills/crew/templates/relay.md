## Message

State the request, question, response, result, or correction. Distinguish
observed facts, proposals, and unresolved questions. For a correction, name
the earlier relay and the precise claim being corrected.

## Evidence

Link the task, prior relays, reports, reviews, or canonical sources actually
read. Include exact source identity when it matters. Do not duplicate large
artifacts or infer approval from another agent's claim.

## Next action

Name the recipient's requested action and what remains blocked or unknown.
Write None with a reason when no action is requested.
